Research Vision & Focus

Core Research Mission Biomechanics • Psycholinguistics • 3D Computer Vision

Toward an Unbiased Taxonomy of Everyday Hand Shapes: Unifying Grasping & Communicative Gestures

Traditional hand biomechanics and upper-limb prosthetics have focused almost exclusively on prehensile object grasping—leaving a major unmet need in natural, conversational gesturing. Working closely with researchers in psycholinguistics alongside biomechanics and 3D computer vision, my long-term research investigates how people actually use their hands throughout daily life to establish an unbiased, data-driven taxonomy of hand postures that treats communicative gestures as equally fundamental as physical manipulation.

Unbiased Hand Shape & Gesture Taxonomy

Capturing unconstrained everyday hand movements to build a comprehensive, unbiased posture taxonomy spanning both prehensile grasps and non-prehensile communicative gestures.

Psycholinguistics & Multimodal Context

Collaborating with psycholinguists to model how hand kinematics, gaze-hand interaction, speech prosody, and social context distinguish communicative gesturing from object manipulation.

Markerless & Egocentric 3D Vision

Combining multi-camera markerless motion capture, egocentric smart-glasses sensing, synthetic datasets, and spatio-temporal graph models to enable expressive, intuitive upper-limb prostheses.
